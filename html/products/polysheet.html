About Our Polysheet Sheets

At Tensorgrid Ltd, we supply Polysheet sheets as an affordable alternative to Perspex, providing reliable performance, good clarity, and versatility for a wide range of fabrication and display applications. Polysheet offers a practical solution where cost efficiency is important while still maintaining durability and ease of use.

Sheet Specifications

Standard Sheet Size: 8 × 4 feet
Clear Polysheet Thickness: 2.5mm – 10mm

Key Features

  • Affordable alternative to Perspex
  • Good optical clarity and smooth surface finish
  • Lightweight and easy to fabricate
  • Easy cutting, bending, and shaping
  • Suitable for indoor and light outdoor applications
  • Consistent quality for general-purpose use
