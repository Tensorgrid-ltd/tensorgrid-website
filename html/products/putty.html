About Premium Putty

At Tensorgrid Ltd, we supply Premium Putty, a high-quality glazing and sealing compound designed for strong adhesion, smooth finishing, and long-lasting performance. It is specially formulated to provide secure fixing and sealing for glass installations while delivering a clean and professional finish.

Premium Putty is easy to apply, durable, and suitable for both residential and commercial applications, making it an ideal solution for glazing, repairs, and general sealing works.
