Aluminium Installation

At Tensorgrid Ltd, we offer comprehensive aluminium solutions for modern construction projects. Our services cover high-quality fittings, durable profiles, and advanced Euro series systems to ensure both functionality and aesthetic appeal.

Aluminium Fittings

We supply and install premium aluminium fittings designed for strength, precision, and durability. Our fittings are ideal for doors, windows, partitions, and curtain walls, providing smooth operation and long-lasting performance. Each fitting is carefully selected to complement the aluminium framework and enhance the overall finish of your project.

Local Aluminium Profile

Our range of locally sourced aluminium profiles is perfect for cost-effective yet high-quality installations. These profiles are versatile, corrosion-resistant, and suitable for a variety of structural and architectural applications. We provide customized solutions tailored to your project’s specifications, ensuring seamless integration and professional results.

Euro Series Aluminium

For modern, high-performance projects, we offer Euro series aluminium systems. These profiles are engineered to meet international standards, combining superior thermal insulation, structural stability, and sleek aesthetics. Ideal for commercial facades, office partitions, and premium residential installations, our Euro series aluminium solutions deliver efficiency, durability, and a contemporary look.
