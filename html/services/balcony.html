Balcony Glass Installation

Modern balcony glass installation solutions for enhanced safety and aesthetics

At Tensorgrid Ltd, we provide modern balcony glass installation solutions designed to enhance safety, visibility, and architectural aesthetics. Our systems create clean, unobstructed views while maintaining strong structural support and durability.

We install frameless and semi-frameless balcony systems using high-quality mounting components to ensure secure fixing, stability, and a sleek contemporary finish.

Installation Systems Include:

  • U-Channel Systems for secure base mounting and minimal visual obstruction
  • Handrail Systems for added safety and structural reinforcement
  • Top Groove Fixing for clean and concealed glass support
  • Top U-Channel Installations for enhanced alignment and stability
  • Spigot Mounting Systems for modern frameless balcony designs

Key Benefits

  • Modern, minimalist appearance
  • Strong and secure glass support systems
  • Clear, uninterrupted views
  • Weather-resistant and durable installations
  • Suitable for residential and commercial balconies
